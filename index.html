For Noor

A letter, written on Saturday night

Noor,

I am sorry. I know I hurt you, and I have not stopped thinking about it.

17 October 2026

I am not writing this to defend myself. There is no good reason for what I did, and I will not hide behind a bad one.

I was careless with something that is precious to me. I let my pride speak louder than my love. I did not listen when you needed me to, and I made you feel unseen. Of everything I have done, that is the part I regret most.

You never asked me to be perfect. You only asked me to be kind, and to be there.

The silence between us has been heavier than any argument. I keep picking up my phone to tell you something small, and then I remember. This page is the only way I could think of to say what I did not say well in person.

I am sorry for the words, for the moments I got wrong, and for every minute you spent upset because of me. You deserve better than that, and I want to be better.

What I miss about us

  • Your voice. The way it changes when you are happy, and the way it softens when you are tired.
  • Your laugh. It can turn the worst day around, and I have not heard it nearly enough lately.
  • How you care. You notice the small things. I should have noticed that more, and thanked you for it.

What I promise you

I will listen first.Before I answer, before I explain, I will hear what you are actually telling me.

I will say it when I am wrong.Quickly, and without making you wait or ask twice.

I will show up.On the good days and on the hard ones, and not only when it is easy.

Noor, will you forgive me?

Take all the time you need. I will be here either way.

Thank you, Noor.

You just made me the happiest person in the world. I do not take your forgiveness lightly, and I will spend every day showing you that it was worth giving.

Come here. The first thing I want is to hold you and say it in person, and then a long evening that is only about you.

Yours, always
